## A. EXP1-S-10 — exact revised wording

**Question:** Someone is showing you how to use an unfamiliar tool. What makes their explanation most useful to you?

- Red: They get me to a working result quickly, then help me improve it.
- Blue: They let me explore what the tool can do through interesting examples.
- Yellow: They build a steady learning partnership and keep a shared commitment from one session to the next.
- Green: They explain how the parts work and why each step matters.

The Yellow response now measures dependable learning partnership, continuity, and shared commitment—not kindness or general teaching competence.

## B. EXP1-S-11 — exact revised wording

**Question:** A close friend is handling a disagreement that affects several people. Which quality in their approach would you respect most?

- Red: They move the issue toward a clear decision that people can act on.
- Blue: They open a new path that gives the situation more room to improve.
- Yellow: They look for a form of continued connection that people can sustain after the disagreement.
- Green: They separate facts, assumptions, and responsibilities before conclusions.

The Yellow response now expresses sustainable relational continuity without presenting Yellow as the uniquely mature conflict response.

## C. EXP1-L-G-02 — exact revised wording
