## Legacy reconstruction findings

- Fairness, honesty, accountability, inclusion, kindness, consideration, loyalty, and confidence were repeatedly treated as color evidence without a governed motivational tradeoff.
- Single-select items frequently reduced colors to taking charge, entertaining, caring, or organizing rather than equally credible motivations.
- Several items plausibly measured an adjacent color, especially Yellow versus Green continuity/predictability and Red versus Blue action/novelty.
- Exact duplicate rows and repeated semantic families inflated apparent bank depth and repeat exposure.
- Blue was often framed through boredom, distraction, impulsivity, or routine intolerance; Green through anxiety, withdrawal, introversion, or discomfort.
- General/cross-context and work/team abstractions dominated, limiting ordinary-life behavioral evidence.
- Adaptability, confidence, accuracy, organization, responsibility, and mature conflict behavior often measured skill or self-image rather than preference.
- Many statements lacked a meaningful alternative or reason why endorsement distinguished the assigned color from the other three.

## Complete expansion backlog

- **EXP-001 — HIGH:** social breadth versus relational depth. Legacy items frequently substitute sociability or introversion; existing candidates establish the distinction but lack enough independent contexts for a large bank.
- **EXP-002 — HIGH:** relational continuity versus structural predictability. Legacy material repeatedly assigns generic caution, reliability, or introversion to these colors without exposing why stability matters.
- **EXP-003 — HIGH:** result ownership versus process reliability. Many legacy questions measure generic responsibility or competence; additional motive-centered parallel items are needed.
- **EXP-004 — HIGH:** preferences in other people. The first 75 legacy questions provide almost no valid PREFERENCE_IN_OTHERS evidence; the few examples are virtue-loaded or semantically unclear.
- **EXP-005 — HIGH:** positive ordinary-life attention and attraction. Reconstruction still contains disproportionate uncertainty, pressure, change, and problem scenarios relative to calm or enjoyable ordinary life.
- **EXP-006 — MEDIUM:** progress-seeking action versus possibility-seeking spontaneity. Legacy action language often conflates Red momentum with Blue spontaneity; more controlled non-work contrasts are needed.
- **EXP-007 — HIGH:** non-virtue relational maintenance and mutual support. Legacy Yellow items frequently claim kindness, consideration, reliability, or organization instead of exposing the relational motive and a reasonable alternative.
- **EXP-008 — MEDIUM:** freedom and variety without distractibility or boredom confounds. Batch 04 contains several Blue items that measure boredom, attention persistence, or inability to tolerate structure rather than a positive preference for possibility and freedom.
- **EXP-009 — MEDIUM:** drains and frustrations anchored in governed motivations. Legacy drain and frustration items rely on boredom, tension, bluntness, anxiety, or accountability virtues rather than showing which governed motivation is blocked.
