## RED proposals

Original: “When a group keeps circling the same decision, I’m willing to name a direction so everyone can react to something concrete.”

Proposed revision: **“When a decision stalls, I’d rather propose a direction and take responsibility for moving it forward than keep gathering more views.”**

Why: naming a direction can be facilitation competency. The revision makes Red decision ownership and progress win a tradeoff against continued consultation.

### `C01-L-R-04` — REVISE

Proposed revision: **“When a plan is losing ground, restoring forward movement matters more to me than perfecting the replacement first.”**

Why: changing direction before agreement relied on an unsupported Red change/risk doctrine. The revision returns to historically established momentum versus preparation.

### `C01-L-R-05` — KEEP

“After I own a mistake, my attention moves quickly to the decision that will get things back on track.”

Why: owning the mistake is neutral framing; the signal is the pull toward the next decision and restored momentum.

## OWNER review checklist

2. Is the tradeoff real and understandable?
3. Could an equally mature person of another color choose differently for a model-consistent reason?
4. Are all answer choices similarly defensible and socially acceptable?
5. Is the claim historically established or OWNER-governed rather than merely inferred?
