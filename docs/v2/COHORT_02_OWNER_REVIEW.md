# VIAGO Cohort 02 — OWNER review

These 24 questions are review-only development candidates. Nothing is active.

## Situational single-select candidates

### 1. `C02-S-01` — volunteer leadership

> A volunteer event is about to begin, but responsibilities are still unclear. What contribution comes most naturally to you first?

- **Red:** Set the first priorities and help people take ownership of them
- **Blue:** Create an engaging way for volunteers to begin contributing
- **Yellow:** Connect with volunteers about where they feel most able to contribute
- **Green:** Map the essential tasks, resources, and order of operations

**Measures:** Leadership through direction, energy, supportive fit, or operational structure.

### 2. `C02-S-02` — customer decision

> A customer requests an important change, but the scope is still unclear. What do you naturally focus on first?

- **Red:** Define the decision that must be made and establish the next step
- **Blue:** Explore creative alternatives that might make the request work
- **Yellow:** Understand which customer and team commitments the change will affect
- **Green:** Clarify the requirements, dependencies, and likely consequences

**Measures:** Closure, possibility, commitments, or evidence when a request is ambiguous.

### 3. `C02-S-03` — travel disruption

> A missed connection changes a group trip with little warning. What matters most in your first response?

- **Red:** Choose the strongest available route so the trip keeps moving
- **Blue:** Look for an unexpected possibility that could still make the trip memorable
- **Yellow:** Coordinate around the travelers’ needs and the commitments already made
- **Green:** Compare timing, constraints, and reliability before choosing the new route

**Measures:** Progress, possibility, people/commitments, or predictable recovery.

### 4. `C02-S-04` — shared household routine

> A shared household routine keeps breaking down. Which response feels most natural to you?

- **Red:** Choose a clearer arrangement and start using it right away
- **Blue:** Try a more flexible approach that makes the routine easier to stay engaged with
- **Yellow:** Talk through what each person can sustain and agree on together
- **Green:** Lay out the tasks, timing, and handoffs in a repeatable system

**Measures:** Action, engaging flexibility, shared sustainability, or repeatable structure.

### 5. `C02-S-05` — neighborhood resources

> A neighborhood project has enough funding for only one improvement. What guides your recommendation first?

- **Red:** Which choice will create the clearest visible result
- **Blue:** Which choice could generate the most participation and fresh energy
- **Yellow:** Which choice best fits how residents want to use the shared space
- **Green:** Which choice has the strongest cost, maintenance, and feasibility case

**Measures:** Visible outcome, participation energy, community fit, or practical evidence.

### 6. `C02-S-06` — new-team norms

> A newly formed team needs a way of working together. What would you most naturally help establish first?

- **Red:** Clear ownership for decisions and the pace needed to deliver
- **Blue:** An open exchange where possibilities and energy can build
- **Yellow:** Shared expectations that support trust and dependable commitments
- **Green:** Defined roles, processes, and checkpoints that keep work coherent

**Measures:** Team leadership through accountability, participation, trust, or structure.

### 7. `C02-S-07` — customer complaint

> A customer raises an urgent complaint that needs a thoughtful response. What do you naturally contribute first?

- **Red:** Take ownership of the resolution and make the next action clear
- **Blue:** Generate response options that could reopen a constructive path
- **Yellow:** Understand the impact on the customer and protect the working relationship
- **Green:** Verify what happened and define a correction that will hold

**Measures:** Resolution ownership, possibility, relationship repair, or reliable diagnosis.

### 8. `C02-S-08` — event disruption

> Weather forces a community event to change at the last minute. What contribution comes most naturally to you?

- **Red:** Choose a workable backup and organize the immediate switch
- **Blue:** Reimagine the event so the changed format still feels lively
- **Yellow:** Coordinate around participant needs and promises already made
- **Green:** Compare the constraints and select the most reliable fallback

**Measures:** Change response through action, reinvention, commitments, or reliability.

### 9. `C02-S-09` — software selection

> A team is choosing between several capable software tools. What matters most in your recommendation?

- **Red:** Which tool will improve output and speed soonest
- **Blue:** Which tool opens the most interesting possibilities for how the team works
- **Yellow:** Which tool best supports the team’s shared needs and working commitments
- **Green:** Which tool has the strongest evidence for reliability, fit, and integration

**Measures:** Tool choice through performance, possibility, team fit, or evidence.

### 10. `C02-S-10` — unfinished idea

> Someone asks for your first reaction to a promising but unfinished idea. Where does your attention naturally go?

- **Red:** The strongest direction and the next move that would advance it
- **Blue:** The possibilities that could make the idea more vivid or engaging
- **Yellow:** How the idea may connect with the people it is meant to serve
- **Green:** The assumptions, details, and consequences that still need testing

**Measures:** Feedback through advancement, possibility, audience connection, or verification.

### 11. `C02-S-11` — recurring process errors

> A familiar work process keeps producing small but avoidable errors. What do you naturally examine first?

- **Red:** The bottleneck that should be changed so performance improves
- **Blue:** Alternative approaches that could make the process work better
- **Yellow:** The handoffs where people may need clearer support or coordination
- **Green:** The pattern of errors and the process control that would prevent them

**Measures:** Process improvement through performance, alternatives, coordination, or control.

### 12. `C02-S-12` — supporting a new colleague

> A new colleague feels overwhelmed by several competing demands. What kind of help comes most naturally to you?

- **Red:** Identify the most important priority and help them take the next action
- **Blue:** Help them see possibilities and regain some energy for the work
- **Yellow:** Listen to what is weighing on them and stay alongside them as they adjust
- **Green:** Organize the demands into clear steps, resources, and dependencies

**Measures:** Useful support through priority, energy, relational presence, or structure.

### 13. `C02-S-13` — unexpected free time

> An afternoon unexpectedly becomes free with no obligations. What are you most naturally drawn to do?

- **Red:** Use the time to make visible progress on something I want to accomplish
- **Blue:** Choose something spontaneous, social, or new that feels energizing
- **Yellow:** Spend the time in a comfortable way with someone I care about
- **Green:** Put something in order or prepare thoughtfully for what is ahead

**Measures:** An unpressured preference for accomplishment, novelty, connection, or order.

### 14. `C02-S-14` — conflicting reports

> A group receives two credible reports that point toward different decisions. What gets your attention first?

- **Red:** Which decision must be made now to keep the objective moving
- **Blue:** Which new explanation or option could connect what the reports reveal
- **Yellow:** Which people and commitments would be affected by choosing either direction
- **Green:** Which evidence, assumptions, and methods account for the disagreement

**Measures:** Closure, possibility, impact, or evidence under conflicting information.

### 15. `C02-S-15` — too many good ideas

> A group has several good ideas but cannot settle on one direction. What contribution comes most naturally to you?

- **Red:** Narrow the choices and recommend the direction most likely to deliver
- **Blue:** Explore whether the strongest ideas can create a more exciting possibility
- **Yellow:** Look for the direction the group can support and sustain together
- **Green:** Define criteria and compare how well each idea meets them

**Measures:** Convergence through outcome, possibility, shared commitment, or criteria.

### 16. `C02-S-16` — settling into a new neighborhood

> You have just moved to an unfamiliar neighborhood. What helps you feel settled first?

- **Red:** Getting the essential tasks handled so daily life is moving
- **Blue:** Exploring interesting places and meeting people nearby
- **Yellow:** Building a few familiar relationships and comfortable routines
- **Green:** Learning the routes, services, schedules, and practical details

**Measures:** Settling through progress, exploration, belonging, or practical clarity.

**Measures:** Motivation from challenge and visible performance standards.

### 18. `C02-L-R-02` — Red

### 19. `C02-L-B-01` — Blue

### 20. `C02-L-B-02` — Blue

### 21. `C02-L-Y-01` — Yellow

**Measures:** Relational trust before rapid closure.

### 22. `C02-L-Y-02` — Yellow

> I feel more settled deepening a dependable relationship than widening my circle with new connections.

**Measures:** Loyal relational depth over broader novelty.

### 23. `C02-L-G-01` — Green

> When several options could work, I feel more confident after comparing the likely consequences than after choosing on instinct.

**Measures:** Evidence and consequence checking over intuition.

### 24. `C02-L-G-02` — Green

> I would rather use a repeatable process that gives consistent results than improvise a different approach each time.

**Measures:** Reliable structure over repeated improvisation.
