# VIAGO 183-Bank Assembler Research

Status: `NON_PRODUCTION_RESEARCH_COMPLETE`

## Executive decision

**183_BANK_READY_FOR_HUMAN_VALIDATION**

Across 30,000 deterministic attempts per bank, the 183-question bank materially improves freshness, balances theoretical color opportunity, represents every whole-person dimension, and produces no exact-family collision within an attempt. The monitored dimensions are sometimes absent from individual attempts, but none fails badly enough to justify more content before human validation. No hard dimension quota is recommended yet.

These multi-attempt values count bank items seen in at least two attempts within the group. Repetition is unavoidable, but the experience is substantially fresher.

## J–K. Selection frequency and bottlenecks

The 183-bank selection-frequency coefficient of variation is **0.1361**, down from 0.7327 in production. The most-selected question appears in 32.36% of attempts; the least-selected appears in 19.27%.

The highest-frequency cluster is driven mainly by the scarcer Yellow Likert pool and by constraints protecting broad-construct/context diversity. The bank contains 23 Red, 27 Blue, 19 Yellow, and 24 Green Likert items, while selecting six of each. Yellow therefore has the highest baseline inclusion pressure (31.58%). This is a pool-depth bottleneck to monitor, not an immediate content-expansion failure.

No question is unselected. Full rank, percentile, count, and rate evidence for all 183 questions is in the machine-readable result.

## L. Semantic collisions

- Exact semantic-family collisions within attempts: **zero by fail-closed selector constraint**.
- Broader construct repeated placements: mean 11.83, median 12, p95 15.
- Broader construct overlap between consecutive attempts: mean 18.13.

The broader grouping is intentionally coarse: a repeated construct can be a permitted parallel measure with different orientation, context, or response format. The selector caps any broad construct at three questions. Human validation should compare cap three with cap two rather than treating all repeated broad groups as defects.
