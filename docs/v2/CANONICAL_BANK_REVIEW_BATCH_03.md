## Coverage observations

- General/cross-context remains overconcentrated: 38 of 75 reviewed identities.
- SELF_PREFERENCE remains the dominant orientation at 39 of 75; both PREFERENCE_IN_OTHERS items reviewed so far are proposed for retirement.
- Relationships/support is the largest reviewed domain, but much of the legacy material confuses Yellow with fairness, helpfulness, reliability, or generic consideration.
- Green legacy signals continue to be contaminated by discipline, anxiety, introversion, and competence; motive-centered clarity and predictability contrasts remain necessary.
- Recovery and positive/enjoyment tones remain thin relative to uncertainty and competing-alternative scenarios.
- Pairwise counts are opportunities, not validated psychometric evidence; RED_GREEN and BLUE_GREEN are most common, while YELLOW_GREEN remains least represented.
