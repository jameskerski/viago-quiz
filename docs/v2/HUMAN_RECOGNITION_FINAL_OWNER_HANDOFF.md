## I. RETEST_NOVELTY_ASSEMBLER_V1 specification

1. History window: three most recent completed attempts. An unfinished attempt contributes only question revisions actually answered; a zero-answer accidental start contributes nothing. Its own manifest remains immutable for resume.
2. Immediately previous logical IDs are hard-excluded when the pool can meet all scoring/composition constraints.
3. Revisions from the three-attempt window receive the strongest soft penalty after the immediate hard exclusion.
4. Recent semantic families are excluded when feasible, then heavily penalized.
5. Recent broad constructs are penalized; within-attempt family cap 1 and construct cap 3 remain.
6. Fallback order: relax prior broad-construct penalty → older-family penalty → older-revision penalty → immediate-family penalty. Never relax composition, six-Likert/color, score opportunity, within-attempt identity uniqueness, attempt security, or immutable provenance. If the immediate-ID exclusion itself is impossible, record the exact relaxation and identities in the manifest; otherwise fail explicitly.
7. Determinism key: bank ID/hash + assembler version + participant-history snapshot hash + seed. Persist the history IDs, policy version, penalty/relaxation diagnostics, question/option revisions, and final manifest hash.
8. Historical versions resolve through immutable logical IDs and frozen metadata. Missing old family/construct metadata falls back conservatively to logical/revision avoidance; it is never guessed from rewritten text.
9. A first attempt has an empty history snapshot and retains current quality/composition behavior.
