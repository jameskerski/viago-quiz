# Cohort 03 — OWNER Review Package

Status: `OWNER_REVIEW_ONLY`  
Governing model: `VIAGO_BEHAVIORAL_COLOR_MODEL_V1_0`  
Composition: 12 single-select + 4 Likert; all 16 are clearly outside workplace/team/project settings  
Runtime impact: **NONE**

Every item below was compared with all 151 active questions and all 40 OWNER-reviewed candidates. Three redundant drafts were rejected before this package.

## C03-S-01

**Scenario/question**

> A personal project you chose has been sitting unfinished for several weeks. What would most naturally draw you back into it?

**Responses and color mapping**

- **Red:** Choosing the next visible milestone and deciding to finish it
- **Blue:** Finding a fresh angle that makes the project interesting again
- **Yellow:** Remembering the commitment I made and who it matters to
- **Green:** Working out why progress stopped and creating a more reliable approach

**What it measures:** What motive naturally restores follow-through after a self-chosen project loses momentum: completion, renewed interest, meaningful commitment, or a reliable method.

**Coverage classification:** `DELIBERATE_PARALLEL_MEASUREMENT`

**Closest existing/new question:** `C01-L-R-05`

**Why this item earns a place:** It adds ordinary-life recovery evidence without asking whether the respondent is responsible or productive; each option offers a credible route back into the project.
