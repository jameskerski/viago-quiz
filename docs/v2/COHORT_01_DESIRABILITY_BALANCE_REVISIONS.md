# Cohort 01 — desirability-balance revisions

## `C01-L-Y-02` — Yellow

> When a workable group decision is ready, I’m more comfortable delaying it to hear from the people most affected than moving ahead and gathering their feedback afterward.

**Balance:** Both hearing people before the decision and moving ahead with later feedback are considerate, workable choices; the distinction is Yellow preference for voice and inclusion before closure.

## `C01-S-01` — one option per color

> A time-sensitive problem appears and no one clearly owns it. What contribution comes most naturally to you first?

- **Red:** Set a workable first direction and coordinate the next action
- **Blue:** Generate a few fast possibilities and rally people around the most promising one
- **Yellow:** Check the immediate effects on people and coordinate the support they will need
- **Green:** Verify the critical facts and define a reliable first response

**Balance:** Every option makes a useful contribution under time pressure; preference separates direction, possibility-generation, people support, and factual reliability.

## `C01-S-07` — one option per color

> Friends are choosing an activity, and the conversation has stalled. What contribution comes most naturally to you?

- **Red:** Narrow the workable choices and recommend one so the group can get going
- **Blue:** Offer a fresh possibility that could bring some energy back to the group
- **Yellow:** Look for the choice that best fits the group’s shared mood and preferences
- **Green:** Compare time, cost, and logistics and suggest the most practical fit

**Balance:** All four options help the group reasonably; Yellow expresses relational fit and harmony without implying that the other options exclude or disregard anyone.
