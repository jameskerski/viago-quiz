# VIAGO Cohort 01 — polished candidate review

These 16 questions are **candidates only**. Nothing here is active or approved for production.

For Likert questions, read the statement as a test taker deciding how strongly it describes you.

## 1. `C01-L-R-01` — Red

> When a decision stalls, I’d rather propose a direction and take responsibility for moving it forward than keep gathering more views.

**Measures:** Red preference for decision ownership and restored progress over continued consultation.

## 2. `C01-L-R-02` — Red

> If a decision can be changed later, I’d rather choose a workable option now than wait for every detail.

**Measures:** Red preference for reversible action and momentum over complete information.

## 3. `C01-L-R-03` — Red

> When time is short, I become more direct about the next action, even if the conversation feels abrupt.

**Measures:** Red focus on immediate action under pressure rather than directness by itself.

## 4. `C01-L-R-04` — Red

> When a plan is losing ground, restoring forward movement matters more to me than perfecting the replacement first.

**Measures:** Red preference for restoring momentum over perfecting a new plan.

## 5. `C01-L-R-05` — Red

> After I own a mistake, my attention moves quickly to the decision that will get things back on track.

**Measures:** Red attention shifting toward the next decision and restored progress.

## 6. `C01-L-B-02` — Blue

> In a room where I don’t know anyone, I usually find a natural way into the conversation without waiting to be introduced.

**Measures:** Blue attraction to broad social connection and spontaneous interaction.

## 7. `C01-L-B-03` — Blue

> When plans change at the last minute, I can usually spot something interesting in the new direction.

**Measures:** Blue attraction to novelty and possibility rather than generic adaptability.

## 8. `C01-L-B-05` — Blue

> In a quiet group, I feel drawn to spark conversation even when there is nothing specific we need to decide.

**Measures:** Blue attraction to conversational energy without a task requirement.

## 9. `C01-L-Y-02` — Yellow

> I have trouble supporting a workable group decision when the people most affected had no voice in it, even if reopening it would slow us down.

**Measures:** Yellow priority for people, voice, and fairness when it costs speed.

## 10. `C01-L-G-01` — Green

> When instructions leave gaps, I’d rather clarify them before starting than make a reasonable assumption and adjust later.

**Measures:** Green preference for clarity over beginning with an adjustable assumption.

## 11. `C01-L-G-02` — Green

> I’m willing to slow down a workable quick fix to check whether it creates another problem later.

**Measures:** Green willingness to trade speed for downstream verification.

## 12. `C01-L-G-03` — Green

> During disagreement, I focus first on separating facts from assumptions, even when others want to settle the issue quickly.

**Measures:** Green preference for evidence separation over rapid social closure.

## 13. `C01-S-01` — one option per color

> A time-sensitive problem appears and no one clearly owns it. What gets your attention first?

- **Red:** Choosing the first action and taking responsibility for it
- **Blue:** Getting people talking so possible responses start to emerge
- **Yellow:** Finding out who is affected and what they need
- **Green:** Confirming the facts and narrowing a workable response

**Measures:** First attention toward ownership, possibility, people impact, or factual clarity under time pressure.

## 14. `C01-S-02` — one option per color

> You join a new project and the goal is still vague. What helps you engage first?

- **Red:** Proposing a starting direction we can adjust as we learn
- **Blue:** Talking through possibilities until an exciting angle emerges
- **Yellow:** Understanding who depends on the project and which commitments matter
- **Green:** Clarifying the requirements and what success must include

**Measures:** Engagement through direction, possibility, people commitments, or requirements.

## 15. `C01-S-03` — one option per color; continue research

> A plan you were counting on changes with little warning. What matters most in your first response?

- **Red:** Finding the best available direction so progress does not stall
- **Blue:** Seeing what new possibilities the change opens up
- **Yellow:** Protecting the people and commitments affected by the change
- **Green:** Rebuilding enough clarity to understand what happens next

**Measures:** Why the respondent responds to change—progress, possibility, people/commitments, or restored clarity. These distinctions are authorized for this item's continued research only, not as general scoring doctrine.

## 16. `C01-S-07` — one option per color

> Friends are choosing an activity, and the conversation has stalled. What contribution comes most naturally to you?

- **Red:** Choose one of the workable ideas so we can get going
- **Blue:** Throw out a fresh idea that gets people excited
- **Yellow:** Find the option that nobody feels pushed out of
- **Green:** Compare time, cost, and logistics before recommending one

**Measures:** A natural contribution of closure, novelty, inclusion, or practical evaluation.
